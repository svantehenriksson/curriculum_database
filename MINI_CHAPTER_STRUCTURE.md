# Mini Chapter Structure

Use one chapter for one clearly defined concept or skill.

## Flow

1. **Hook / Why**
   - Start with a short relatable situation, question, or need for the concept.

2. **Illustration**
   - Show the idea visually before introducing formal notation.
   - Prefer simple, memorable illustrations with low cognitive load.

3. **Idea in words**
   - Explain the concept plainly: what is happening and why.

4. **Mathematics / notation**
   - Introduce the formal rule, symbols, and terminology only after the intuition is clear.

5. **Worked example**
   - Show one or two clean step-by-step examples.

6. **Focused practice**
   - Exercises should practice only the chapter’s main idea at first.
   - Increase difficulty gradually without introducing unrelated concepts.

7. **Application / mastery check**
   - End with a small application, less-scaffolded problem, or self-check.

## Core principles

- **Why before how**
- **Language → image → symbol**
- **One chapter = one topic**
- **Low cognitive load**
- **Visible progress**
- **One memorable hook or illustration is enough**
