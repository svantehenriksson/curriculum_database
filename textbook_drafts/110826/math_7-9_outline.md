## Conservative Gamification Notes

<!--
We keep gamification selective because local curricula emphasize mathematical reasoning, communication, and responsibility; over-gamification would dilute that.
Use games mainly where they increase practice quality, feedback speed, or motivation in conceptually hard transitions.
-->

- Recommended "light gamification" density: ~1 in 6 to 1 in 8 topics.
- Prefer short loops:
  - React mini-tool/challenge (5-10 min)
  - quick physical station with cheap materials
- Keep scoring secondary to explanation quality.
